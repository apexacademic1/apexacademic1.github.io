How it works

Applying is simple.

  1. Fill out the application. Tell us about your background and the subjects you teach best.
  2. Upload your documents. Add a transcript screenshot and your resume.
  3. We review and reach out. If it looks like a good fit, we'll contact you about next steps.

What you'll need

Tutors must have a 3.5+ cumulative GPA. Have these ready before you start:

  • A screenshot of your unofficial transcript showing your cumulative GPA
  • Your resume (PDF or Word)
  • The subjects and grade levels you're comfortable teaching
  • Your general weekly availability

Common questions

Why do I need to upload a transcript?

We use it only to confirm the 3.5+ GPA requirement. An unofficial transcript screenshot is fine.

Why do I have to sign in with Google?

Our application uses Google Forms, which requires a Google sign-in to upload files. Your documents are used only to verify eligibility.

Which subjects can I apply to teach?

We tutor a range of subjects for grades 7-12, including math, science, English, history, and test prep. The form lets you pick every subject you're strong in.

What happens after I apply?

We review every application and email you if it looks like a good match.
